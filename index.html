🤖 Military Rover

An autonomous surveillance, detection and reconnaissance robot concept for remote observation in difficult environments.

🚙 6-Wheel All-Terrain SystemFour main wheels and two middle wheels provide stable movement on rough terrain.
📡 Radar SystemUses ultrasonic sensing to detect nearby objects and measure their distance.
📷 Camera SystemProvides remote visual observation of the surrounding area.
🌙 Night/Low-Light DetectionHelps the rover observe environments when visibility is low.
📍 GPS NavigationTracks the rover’s location and supports navigation during operation.
🌡️ Climate SensorMonitors environmental conditions such as temperature and humidity.
🌧️ Rain SensorDetects rainfall and helps monitor changing weather conditions.
☀️ Auto-Rotatable Solar PanelAdjusts the panel toward sunlight to improve solar-energy collection.
🔋 Rechargeable BatteryStores electrical energy to power the rover and its electronic systems.
📡 Wireless CommunicationEnables remote communication and control between the rover and operator.
🧭 Navigation SystemHelps the rover move toward a destination while avoiding obstacles.
🤖 Autonomous MovementCan be designed to make basic movement decisions using sensors and programmed logic.
🧠 AI Navigation ConceptAI can be used to analyze sensor information and assist with navigation.
🌍 Environmental MonitoringSensors can collect information about the surrounding environment.
🛞 Obstacle DetectionDetects objects in the rover’s path to help prevent collisions.
📦 Modular DesignSensor and electronics modules can be added or changed according to the mission.
🛰️ Remote SurveillanceAllows an operator to observe and monitor an area from a distance.
💡 LED/Status IndicatorsShows operating conditions and system status to the user.
⚡ Power Management SystemManages power distribution between the battery, solar panel, motors, and electronics.
🛡️ Protective Rover BodyProvides a sturdy structure to protect the internal electronics during outdoor operation.
